MotionRanks Publisher: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

MotionRanks Publisher is a small private tool run by one person, the owner of the MotionRanks channel. It publishes the owner's own videos to the owner's own Facebook Page and Instagram account through Meta's APIs. It is not offered to the public, and no one other than the owner signs in to it.

What data the tool handles

Only the owner's own account access: the permissions the owner grants the app for the MotionRanks Facebook Page and the @motionranks Instagram account. The tool uses them to upload and schedule videos and to read back the status of those posts.

What it does not do

It does not collect, store, sell or share personal information about visitors, followers, commenters or any other person. It does not run advertising or tracking, and it has no website, database or server of its own.

Where access tokens are kept

Access tokens for the owner's own accounts are stored only on the owner's own computer, in a file that only the owner can read. They are never sent anywhere except to Meta, to make the requests described above.

Data deletion

Because the tool holds no data about anyone but its owner, there is nothing to delete for other people. The owner can remove the app's access at any time under Settings, Business integrations on Facebook, and by deleting the app on Meta for Developers.
